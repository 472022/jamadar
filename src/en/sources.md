## The family album

A bound scrapbook album, compiled over decades, pairing hand-written Marathi captions with pasted photographs documenting union meetings, government visits, and civic events. Several pages are countersigned by the Deputy Education Officer, Kolhapur, suggesting the album was maintained as something close to an official record.

## Original photographs

A small number of loose, higher-resolution original prints held separately by the family, including the National Award ceremony photograph and a 1973 studio print from Chhaya Photo Studio, Kagal.

## A note on accuracy

Where a caption in the source material was faded, rotated on the page, or otherwise not fully legible, this site says so directly rather than filling the gap with a guess. If you can help verify or complete any part of this record, please get in touch via the family — see the [Tribute](/en/tribute/) page.
