## National Award for Teachers

The high point of Jamadar's public recognition was the **National Award for Teachers**, presented to him at the hands of the **President of India, Giani Zail Singh**, on a stage banner reading "National Awards ... Satyapakon ko Rashtriya ... kar" (सत्यापकों को राष्ट्रीय पुरस्कार). The family holds this photograph both as a loose original print and as two separately framed copies — a sign of how significant this honour was considered within the family.

## Local felicitations

Long before the National Award, Jamadar was repeatedly honoured within his own teaching community — garlanded at receptions marking his various union roles, and by institutions he was associated with, including the teachers' own cooperative bank.
